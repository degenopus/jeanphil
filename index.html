The Story

02 / ABOUT

Jean Phil began as a massive viral sensation on TikTok: an exaggerated, stylized French persona, clad in a distinctive striped suit, famous for his signature catchphrase «Oui Madame». The meme grew into a universe — and the universe became a collection of 2222 unique characters on Robinhood Chain.

The collection lives and evolves. Every day, the art of the floor Jean Phils changes thematically — the lowest-ranked characters evolve into new art, so the collection never stands still and every holder watches their Jean Phil grow. A separate line of limited «OG» editions drops one-of-one event pieces that never enter the main collection.

Jean Phil is a community-first project: an active holder base, constant contests and activities on X, and real rewards for the most active members — winners receive customized, personalized Jean Phil NFTs with special traits.

Jean Phil is an independent community art project. The collection is not directly affiliated with the creator of the original coin — it is an art derivative that stands entirely on its own.
